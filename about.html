Our story

Yenstone has been building in stone since 1991, and we are still doing it the same way: by hand, one stone at a time.

We work from Perhams Farm at Yenston, near Templecombe. Dry stone walling is where we started, and the same patience and care go into all of the landscaping we do today. Patrick developed a passion for it over many years after branching away from general building. It is slow, skilled work that rewards doing it properly.

Everything else we build, from steps and terraces to paving and whole gardens, is shaped by that way of working. We plan the surfaces, the walls and the planting together, in local stone, so the finished garden feels settled from the first season.

Questions we’re often asked

A little more about who we are and how we work. If yours is not here, call us and we will talk it through.

How long have you been trading?

Since 1991. We began with dry stone walling and have built on that ever since.

Where are you based?

At Perhams Farm in Yenston, just outside Templecombe. We work across Somerset, Dorset and Wiltshire.

Are you qualified?

Yes. Patrick is a certified dry stone waller, and Yenstone is a member of the Dry Stone Walling Association.

What do you do?

Dry stone walling and landscaping, including lime mortar pointing, steps and terraces, paving and patios, and groundworks. We also do septic tank installation, bespoke ironmongery and stone cladding.

Do you use local stone?

Wherever we can. We match the stone of your house and your lane, so new work sits naturally beside the old.

Do you do insurance work?

Yes, we do insurance work on walls. Tell us what happened and we will explain what is involved and give you a quote you can pass to your insurer.
