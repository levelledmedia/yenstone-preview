Questions we’re often asked

Straight answers on getting in touch and what happens next. If yours is not here, call us and we will talk it through.

What is the quickest way to reach you?

Call us on 01963 371123. We will answer if we can, and call you back if we are on site. You can also ring the mobile on 07751 278363 or email info@yenstonewalling.co.uk.

What should I tell you when I get in touch?

Tell us about your property and what you have in mind. Photos help, but they are not essential. If you use the form, a name and phone number are all we need to call you back.

What happens after I get in touch?

We arrange a visit to look at the site, measure up and talk through the options. You then get a written quote with the work and the price set out clearly.

Do you charge for quotes?

No, our quotes are free.

How quickly will you get back to me?

We will be in touch as soon as we can. If you reach us while we are on site, we will call you back.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Somerset, Dorset and Wiltshire from our base at Yenston, near Templecombe, including Wincanton, Sherborne, Yeovil, Gillingham, Shaftesbury, Mere and Warminster.
