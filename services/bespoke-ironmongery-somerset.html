Ironwork made for the place it is going

The right metalwork finishes a garden: a gate that closes properly, a feature that picks up the style of the house, details that match the stone around them. Because it is custom-made, it can be designed around your garden rather than adapted to fit it.

We build the landscaping and stonework as well, so the ironwork, the walls and the surfaces can be planned together and fit as one piece.

What we make

Every piece starts with a conversation about where it is going and how it will be used.

Gates and gate furniture

Custom-made gates, hinges and latches designed to suit the opening, the wall or hedge around it and the style of the property.

Garden metalwork

Features and supports for the garden that suit the planting and the stonework around them.

Fittings and details

Brackets, fixings and other small pieces made to match the rest of the job.

Made to match

Ironwork designed alongside new landscaping and stonework, or to complement a garden that already exists.
