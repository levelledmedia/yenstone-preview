Surfaces that last start below the surface

Most paving problems start under the stone, not in it. A patio or drive that sinks, rocks or holds water has usually been laid on ground that was not prepared properly. We dig out, build up the base and set the levels so water runs where it should before any stone goes down.

Because we build walls, steps and gardens as well, we think about how a surface meets everything around it: the edge of a lawn, the foot of a wall, the threshold of a door. The result looks like part of the garden rather than something added to it.

What we lay

We work in natural stone and in gravel, and we will talk you through which suits your house, your budget and the way you use the space.

Patios and terraces

Places to sit, eat and look at the garden, laid to fall gently away from the house and set in stone that suits it.

Paths and walkways

Stone paths that link the house, the garden and the gate, wide enough to use and laid so they stay level.

Drives and forecourts

Gravel and stone drives built on a proper sub-base, with edging that keeps everything in place.

Repairs and relaying

Sunken, uneven or stained paving lifted, the base put right and the stone relaid, reusing what we can.
