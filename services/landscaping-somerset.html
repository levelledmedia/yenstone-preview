Plan the whole garden, then build it in order

Some gardens need a tidy-up. Others need a fresh start: new levels, a proper terrace, steps that make sense, borders that frame the view instead of hiding it. We look at how you use the space and how the land falls before we start.

We plan the paving, steps, walls and planting together, so it feels like one garden rather than a set of separate jobs. Then we agree the order of work, so each stage leads naturally into the next.

What we landscape

A landscaping job can be as simple as a new path or as full as a whole-garden scheme. It always starts with a visit.

Hard landscaping

Terraces, paths, steps, walls and surfaces that give the garden its structure, built in stone that suits the house.

Garden features

Seating areas, raised beds, water features and focal points that give a garden character and somewhere to look.

Outdoor improvements

Lawns, borders and awkward levels put right, so the space you have works better for the way you use it.

Bespoke projects

Something particular in mind? We work from your ideas and the site to design and build something made for your garden.

Not sure where to start? Call us. We will come and look at the garden, talk through what you want and explain how we would approach it.
