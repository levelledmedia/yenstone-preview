The work you do not see afterwards

Good groundworks are what everything else rests on: the levels, the drainage and the foundations. If they are right, walls, paving and gardens stay where they should. If they are not, nothing built on top will last.

We dig, level and prepare the ground ourselves, so it is ready for what comes next, whether that is a terrace, a drive, a wall or a whole garden.

What we do

From a small garden to a whole plot, we start by looking at the ground and what it needs to carry.

Site preparation and excavation

Clearing, digging out and levelling ground for terraces, paths, drives and building work.

Foundations

Firm, correctly built bases for walls, steps and paved areas.

Drives and hardstanding

Prepared and surfaced for vehicles, with drainage that keeps them dry.

Drainage

Getting water away from where you do not want it.
